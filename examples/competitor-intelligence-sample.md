## Signal briefs

### 1. Fountain publishes a new frontline hiring study

- **Competitor:** Fountain
- **Primary category:** Market research / content narrative
- **Event date:** 27 October 2026 (Fountain newsroom announcement); report page says published 29 October 2026
- **Region:** United States
- **Sources:** [Fountain announcement](https://www.fountain.com/news/fountain-report-frontline-hiring-millions-apply-few-hired) · [Report page](https://resources.fountain.com/reports/louder-than-words-frontline-workforce-report-2026)
- **Evidence:** Fountain says the report combines analysis of 12.5 million applications on its platform during 2025 with a June 2026 survey of 1,014 U.S. frontline workers. The company reports an overall hire rate of about 8% and says roughly 881,000 applications timed out without a clear yes or no.
- **Why it may matter:** The report puts candidate communication and application closure into the frontline hiring conversation, alongside volume and speed. It gives the market a new, highly visible data point and content narrative.
- **TurboHire relevance:** **Relevant to monitor.** It overlaps with the problem space around candidate continuity and hiring execution. It is not evidence of a new Fountain product capability or a universal frontline hiring benchmark.
- **Confidence:** **High** that Fountain published these findings; **limited** for generalizing them beyond Fountain’s platform and the report’s U.S. sample.
- **Open questions:** Review the report methodology, definitions of “application” and “timeout,” and the differences among industry funnels before reusing any figure.
- **Review status:** Needs human review before external use.

## Feed quality observations

- The file contains **172 items** dated from **8 May 2013 to 11 November 2026**. **152 of 172** are more than 90 days old, so the current output is not a recent-news-only feed.
- The first result for “Skillate / Sense” concerns youth cadets in Zimbabwe. Other results use “sense” in unrelated contexts, including sports, science, and generic news. A competitor label on an RSS item is not proof that the article is about that competitor.
- Some relevant stories appear more than once under different publishers or headlines. Treat these as one underlying signal when they report the same event.
- Several feed records are labeled `general_competitor_news`, even when the article is an analyst mention, market study, or unrelated false match. Reclassify after opening and checking the source.

## Recommended workflow improvements

1. Apply a default 90-day view, with an archive option for older stories.
2. Remove broad standalone matching for **“Sense”**; use verified company names, product names, domains, and context terms for Skillate/Sense.
3. Add a human relevance check before a result becomes a competitive signal.
4. Deduplicate by canonical destination URL and event, not only by headline.
5. Keep signal type, source verification, and PMM interpretation as separate fields.
