# TurboHire positioning

## Category

**Frontline Hiring with Automation and AI.**

Frontline hiring means building the workforce that directly supports an organization's operations, customer delivery, or revenue. It can include blue-collar and grey-collar roles, hourly roles, revenue-facing teams, and high-volume white-collar operational roles.

Frontline hiring and high-volume hiring overlap, but they are not interchangeable. *Frontline* describes the workforce and its relationship to business operations. *High-volume* describes a scale or operating condition.

## Audience problem

In many enterprise environments, hiring demand is continuous, distributed, and concurrent. Multiple roles, locations, candidates, and stakeholders move through the hiring process at the same time. The operating strain often appears between stages: context, feedback, approvals, communication, and next actions do not move together automatically. Recruiters spend time coordinating this movement, and delays can propagate into later steps.

Describe the mechanism in the specific hiring scenario. Do not claim every enterprise hiring process has the same bottleneck.

## Solution narrative

TurboHire helps enterprises execute frontline hiring with automation and AI. **SuperAgent is the execution layer** that coordinates configured workflow activity and supports candidate progression. Hiring teams retain responsibility for assessment standards, judgment, feedback, approvals, exceptions, and hiring decisions.

The simple narrative sequence is:

1. Explain the workforce or business requirement.
2. Show where hiring complexity or handoffs affect execution.
3. Explain how automation and AI support the process.
4. Show where people remain accountable.
5. Connect the operating change to a relevant business outcome, without claiming an unmeasured result.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Category:** Frontline Hiring with Automation and AI.
2. **Product:** SuperAgent, the hiring execution layer.
3. **Mechanism:** AI Agents support evaluation against human-defined criteria; Automation carries out configured workflow actions; GenAI Agents support communication; Human Inputs retain control.
4. **Value:** continuous hiring execution, recruiter productivity at scale, and speed with enterprise control.
5. **Business relevance:** workforce availability and readiness for operations, service delivery, or revenue work.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not lead with ATS, generic AI platform, or generic orchestration platform language.
- Do not use “self-driving hiring” as the market category. It may appear only when a current approved brief uses it to describe a capability or campaign concept.
- Do not imply that SuperAgent makes final hiring decisions or removes recruiter accountability.
- Do not promise that technology creates candidate supply, guarantees hiring speed, or guarantees business or revenue results.
- Do not use frontline positioning where it does not fit the role or audience, such as specialist leadership hiring, unless the brief explains the connection.
- “Hiring velocity” refers to movement through the process and its dependencies, not simply recruiter speed.

## Optional short description

TurboHire supports enterprise frontline hiring with automation and AI. SuperAgent coordinates hiring execution while recruiters and hiring teams retain control of evaluation, approvals, and decisions.
