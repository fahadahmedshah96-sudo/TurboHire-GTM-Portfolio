# TurboHire positioning

## Category

TurboHire works in Frontline Hiring with Automation and AI.

Frontline hiring includes roles that directly support operations, customer delivery, or revenue at scale. It can include blue-collar, grey-collar, revenue-facing, and high-volume white-collar roles.

## Product

SuperAgent is TurboHire's execution layer for hiring. It coordinates configured workflow activity across hiring stages while keeping recruiters and other decision-makers in control.

## Operating model

- AI Agents evaluate, match, score, and prioritize against human-defined criteria.
- Automation carries out configured, repeatable workflow actions.
- GenAI Agents support communication and content-related work.
- Human Inputs retain judgment, approvals, exceptions, and hiring decisions.

Recruiter Copilot provides recruiter-facing insights and analysis. Do not describe it as executing workflows.

## Outcomes

Connect product capabilities to operational outcomes such as candidate progression, hiring throughput, recruiter capacity, staffing coverage, and time to productive hire.

Use revenue impact only when the causal link and evidence are clear.

## Guardrails

- Do not position TurboHire primarily as an ATS or as a generic AI platform.
- Do not claim AI makes final hiring decisions.
- Do not invent product capabilities, integrations, metrics, or customer results.
- Use “SuperAgent” exactly.
- Prefer “process” over “journey.”
