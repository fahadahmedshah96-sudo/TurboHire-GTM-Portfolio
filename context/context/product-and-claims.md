# Product facts and claim rules

## Source order

1. Current TurboHire product materials or user-provided sources
2. Current approved TurboHire website and public materials
3. Older reference material, used only when newer information is unavailable

## Claim status

Mark product statements as one of:

- Verified and current
- Needs confirmation
- Demo or illustrative
- Roadmap or under development

Do not present demo, planned, or unverified capabilities as generally available.

## Evidence

For each metric, customer outcome, integration, or material product claim, record the source and date. If evidence is missing, omit the claim or mark it for review.
