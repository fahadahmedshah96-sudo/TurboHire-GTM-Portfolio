# TurboHire voice and format rules

## Shared voice

- Calm, clear, practical, and evidence-led.
- Explain the operating mechanism and its consequence.
- Use concrete language and natural sentence variation.
- Avoid hype, rhetorical questions, em dashes, forced contrasts, and generic AI claims.

## Blogs

Use the TurboHire blog framework. Substantial blogs need a practical example and a dedicated TurboHire section. Include the comparison table where relevant. Metrics and FAQs are optional.

## eBooks

Use a sustained, research-led argument. Prefer primary sources for factual claims and explain the operating mechanism behind the topic.

## Sales and marketing decks

Define the audience, commercial thesis, evidence, and job of each slide before designing. Use TurboHire’s established visual system: #6E3EFE accent, Archivo headings, and Manrope body text where available.
