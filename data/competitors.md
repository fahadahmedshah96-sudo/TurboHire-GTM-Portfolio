# TurboHire Competitor Monitoring Registry

## Purpose

Track public market signals that may affect TurboHire's positioning, product narrative, or go-to-market activity.

This is a working competitor set based on public product overlap. It does not imply that every company competes with TurboHire in every deal.

## Monitoring rules

### Signal types

Track:

- Acquisitions and mergers
- Funding rounds, investments, and investor announcements
- Strategic partnerships and integrations
- Product launches and major product changes
- AI and automation announcements
- Customer wins and case studies
- Leadership changes
- Geographic or industry expansion
- Regulatory, security, or compliance announcements

### Source priority

1. Official company newsroom, product blog, investor page, or press release
2. Official company LinkedIn page
3. Google News search feed for discovery
4. Credible business or HR technology publication

Google News results are discovery signals. Verify material claims against an official company announcement or a credible publication.

LinkedIn pages are included for monitoring and review. Automated retrieval of competitor posts through LinkedIn's API may require approved access.

### Record for every signal

- Headline
- Company and product
- Signal type
- Publication date
- Source name and URL
- Market or hiring segment
- Short factual summary
- Potential implication for TurboHire
- Verification status: `verified`, `reported`, or `unverified`
- Human review status: `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected`

Do not present an implication as an approved TurboHire position until a person has reviewed it.
