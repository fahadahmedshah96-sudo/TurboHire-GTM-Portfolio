# Product context and claim rules

## Product description

TurboHire is focused on Talent Acquisition. It can operate alongside enterprise HRMS, HCM, ATS, assessment, background-verification, and communication systems. Do not describe TurboHire as a replacement for the customer's HRMS or system of record unless an approved, current source explicitly says otherwise.

**SuperAgent** is TurboHire's hiring execution layer. It supports progression across configured hiring workflows. Recruiters and hiring teams retain accountability for criteria, decisions, approvals, and exceptions.

## Architecture language

When explaining the architecture, include the four layers and keep their roles distinct:

| Layer | Describe it as | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Agents | Evaluate, match, score, or prioritize candidates using human-defined criteria or weightages. | Do not describe a model as deciding who should be hired. Confirm the exact input signals and feature availability. |
| Automation | Carries out configured workflow actions and moves work through the process. | Do not imply every action is automatic or available in every customer setup. |
| GenAI Agents | Support communication and assistance, such as drafting or candidate communications, when available in the relevant product. | Confirm release status and region before describing a specific capability. |
| Human Inputs | Preserve human judgment, feedback, approvals, exception handling, and hiring decisions. | Keep the accountable human role explicit. |

## Recruiter Copilot

Recruiter Copilot is an analysis and insight-support layer for recruiters. SuperAgent executes configured workflow actions; Recruiter Copilot surfaces information and helps recruiters interpret pipeline or candidate context. Do not say Copilot performs actions or change the product boundary unless a current approved source confirms an update. Verify its release status before making an availability claim.

## Capability areas to verify before use

Source material references candidate intake, resume parsing and structured data, screening and scoring, ranking, candidate database search or reuse, interview workflows, scheduling, approvals, follow-ups, candidate communication, offer activity, verification, and pre-onboarding. These are capability areas, not a blanket statement that every capability is generally available in every market or configuration.

Before naming a feature, confirm:

- current release status (generally available, beta, pilot, planned, or retired);
- geography and language availability;
- workflow or integration prerequisites;
- whether the action is automated, assisted, or requires human approval;
- exact approved product terminology.

## Claims register

| Claim type | Default status | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Category and core product framing | Approved baseline for this portfolio context | Current positioning brief or current approved public page |
| Architecture roles and human accountability | Approved baseline, verify detailed implementation wording | Current product or architecture material |
| Specific feature availability | Verify before use | Current release notes, product documentation, or approved product owner confirmation |
| Customer names, logos, and testimonials | Do not include by default | Public customer permission or an already-approved public asset |
| Performance metrics, ROI, and customer outcomes | Do not include without verification | Exact source, date, definition, cohort, geography, and permission to publish |
| Competitor comparisons | Research-only until checked | Dated primary evidence, fair attribution, and review before external use |
| Integrations, security, compliance, and regional claims | Verify for the named market and product version | Current official documentation or approved legal/product source |

No performance numbers are approved by this file. Treat any number found in older decks, reports, drafts, or conversations as a lead to verify, not a ready-to-publish claim.
